# Moving dogs and uninterrupted care

The owner rejected standing pictures translating through the park, then reported a half-second reload-like flash after care actions. The five approved household dogs now have authored side, front and rear movement; the main screen uses the same motion sets for wandering, zoomies, approaching and turning. Sleep, illness, training, feeding, affection, saved progress and the procedural3D alternative retain their existing paths.

## Animation

Built-in imagegen produced identity-referenced sheets in `design/reference/locomotion/`: eight side strides plus four views; four whole-body gallop keys (extension, landing, gather, push); four front and four rear stride drawings. Source references retain Expo's tricolor mask/blaze/ruff, Mali's shorter plumper body, Oreo's pied coat/bat ears, Paolo's folded ears/dark jowls/tongue and Hachiku's cream Akita likeness. The prompt briefs are recorded beside those sheets. The lower leg region completes a reflected diagonal exchange where a generated front sheet repeated the same supporting foreleg, without reflecting the face, ear asymmetry or upper coat.

[nihui's MIT-licensed RIFE ncnn Vulkan](https://github.com/nihui/rife-ncnn-vulkan) 20221029 portable executable and upstream RIFE4.6 weights supply offline in-betweens. This is authored 2D animation, not a rotatable3D dog or a physically exact skeletal simulation. There is no model/runtime service in the browser and no Meshy subscription. The displayed atlas contains112 frames per dog:32run,16walk,32turn,16toward and16away. Ground contact/suspension drawings register to one floor; stride advances with actual root travel, acceleration/braking and eased heading. The pond rejects actual footsteps as well as tap destinations. Greetings interrupt movement and retain the sniff rig; petting uses the approved affection drawing.

RIFE directory mode uses `input_count / output_count`, not endpoint-inclusive duration. With four keys plus a loop endpoint and eight samples per segment, request40 outputs and retain32. Requesting33 produced five held final frames while the root kept moving; pixel comparisons caught that defect. `test-motion-art.cjs` checks the loop seam, all consecutive run intervals and distinct lower-leg silhouettes in both depth directions for all five dogs.

Large generic limb warps were rejected after recordings showed stretched knees. Full-opacity interpolated drawings carry the major anatomy; the inherited skin supplies secondary motion. Running heads retain the approved larger proportions. The builder records actual foreground bounds after the same head warp used by the shader. The hero fits those bounds and reserves the measured control-group rectangle in current camera units; a guessed world-unit margin clipped the muzzle on short phones. Motion honors reduced motion, pauses while hidden, disposes textures/listeners and targets30display frames/s.

One high-resolution320px-tile atlas loads for the selected dog;192px tiles serve companions/limited texture-size devices. The hero defers its optional atlas until the standing dog is usable. Each atlas has a480×576 alpha-only touch mask (48px/tile), independent of the displayed resolution. Reading every full-size sheet for hit detection caused a cold-load stall; the small masks remove that avoidable main-thread work. Runtime asset files are under `public/dogs/motion/`; source/intermediate sheets never enter the build.

## The care-action flash

An isolated live-browser trace retained the same canvas through petting, but recorded canvas width/height writes outside animation callbacks as the feedback row changed height. A resize clears the WebGL drawing buffer before the next scheduled render. Both illustrated renderers now let ResizeObserver mark a pending resize and apply it inside the animation task that draws the replacement frame. Unchanged dimensions do nothing. The feedback row no longer animates its height. Normal actions do not navigate, create another renderer or fetch the artwork again.

`test-action-continuity.cjs` instruments the real canvas setters and requestAnimationFrame. Pet/play/clean, tip changes and park petting must retain the same canvas, keep advancing frames and perform every buffer resize inside a drawing callback. Come closer, Turn around and Zoomies must render multiple artwork frames. Independent review also exercised Feed and Bed, retaining the same canvas and one atlas request across actions.
