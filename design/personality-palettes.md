# Dog game: personality palettes

Intent: a companion game with the dog as the focus, compact icon-led controls and legible care feedback.
Relationship: light warm carriers sit against a quieter personality field and one deep action ink; these are game interpretations, not diagnoses of the real dogs.

Consulted Palette's PALETTE-FIELD-GUIDE.md and its actual `content.js` / `palette-tools.js` / `app.js` search over `data/colors.json`. Verified source combinations:

| Dog | Personality direction | Palette combination | Source colors |
|---|---|---|---|
| Expo | Observant, gentle guardian | 294 | Sulpher Yellow, Cream Yellow, Cossack Green, Salvia Blue |
| Mali | Affectionate, soft and grounded | 55 | Old Rose, White |
| Oreo | Curious, playful, bright | 60 | Pale Lemon Yellow, Dark Tyrian Blue |
| Paolo | Sleepy, warm, food-loving | 11 | Ivory Buff, Mineral Gray |
| Hachiku | Quiet, loyal, patient | 50 | Ivory Buff, Dusky Madder Violet |

Roles: pale paper is the ground; a low-chroma personality field holds the stage; deep matching ink carries text and action; actual warning/sickness colors remain semantic.
Budget: ground and readable neutrals dominate, the stage owns the personality color, selection and focus use sparse deep ink.
Risks: low-contrast pastel text, icon ambiguity and color-only state. Proof: measure actual text/background contrast, retain accessible names/tooltips and a visible control legend, inspect all five palettes at phone/tablet/desktop.

These are production adaptations of credited digital conversions, not exact printed Wada inks. The owner's explicit request for five personality-based game schemes supersedes the prior monochrome/amber console default. Palette data credits Matt DesLauriers / Dain M. Blodorn Kim; see the MIT notice in `palette-data-notice.md`.
