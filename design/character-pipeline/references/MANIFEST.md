|---|---|---|---|

| `oreo-frenchie` | `oreo` | Pied dark & cream French bulldog | `public/dogs/oreo.webp` |
| `paolo-pug` | `paolo` | Fawn pug with dark mask | `public/dogs/paolo.webp` |
| `shiba-chai` | `hachiku` | Shiba ginger with cream urajiro | `public/dogs/hachiku.webp` |
