# Commons: a playable illustrated park

Problem reproduced from the owner's screenshot: perspective distance28 makes Expo a dot; fog/checkerboard ground and isolated cuboids do not make a dog world. Four simultaneous descriptions (help, selected identity, social alert, first-tip) consume the frame. A world-height budget passed while the animal was unusably small.

Design read: a small storybook dog park, using the household's existing cel illustrations and articulated rig. Reference: Nintendogs' readable nearby companions and direct-touch petting, with the current household art as the visual source. The dog and nearby dogs win; shade trees, a footpath, pond, bench and bowl give the walk destinations. These are scene objects, not interface decoration.

Primary renderer becomes an orthographic illustrated world: reusable animated dog shader, no perspective/fog/ground-plane edge, alpha-aware hit tests, bounded zoom .85–1.5, and actual adult dog widths at least120CSSpx at widest zoom. Puppy scale stays distinct with a touchable floor. Household companions are close enough to encounter; existing local neighbours/imported visitors remain in the roster/world. Ground taps and WASD move only the owner's dog. Petting another dog animates that neighbour only; canonical care and social progress still use the existing atomic mutation callbacks.

The procedural3D scene is preserved as an explicitly selected fallback, not the primary product. Preserve import/clear, roster, local simulated chat, keyboard/touch, reduced motion, selected pet and error handling. No file collapse or live-canvas deletion.

Geometry: world at least70% of available park frame in default phone state and over half with first-tip/roster/chat. One compact footer groups selected name+pet, dogs and chat; remove repeated breed/owner-kind box and permanent help band. Social encounters report at the actual encounter, without an alert bar. Real import errors retain visible feedback. First-use guidance remains below the world and has one copy.

Verification must exercise default, minimum/maximum zoom, focused neighbour, movement, pet strokes, actual proximity greetings, imports/clear and every drawer at390×844/390×667,1024×768,1440×900. Check actual on-screen animal size, alpha hit/miss, visibility and touch targets, not just canvas height. Restore the old zoom-out scale in a local fixture and require the dog-size budget to reject it. Independent pixel/interaction review before commit/push/deploy and live verification.
