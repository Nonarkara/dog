# Training the moving dog

Goal: the player cues once, watches a visible canine action finish, marks that actual moment, then rewards it. The scoring clock must follow the renderer, including multi-pose down/paw transitions, instead of assuming every drawing finishes after 500ms. Come, fetch and leave it need visible actions; stay and wait need a held position. Sleeping, unwell or interrupted practice must not produce phantom trials. Existing art, palettes, controls and saved real logs remain.

Implementation order: (1) remove invented enrollment facts and clarify game-scoring guidance from primary reward-based sources; (2) add request IDs and rendered completion acknowledgements, with bounded semantic motion using the existing drawings; (3) exercise all eight positive training paths, early/late/no-marker outcomes, cancellation, reduced motion and old companions; (4) independent review, viewport proof, commit/push/deploy and live verification. A failed renderer/request cancels practice rather than claiming the dog performed.

Primary method reference: [Dogs Trust reward training](https://www.dogstrust.org.uk/dog-advice/training/techniques/positive-reinforcement-training-with-rewards), especially timely reinforcement and rewarding each successful response while learning; [sit training](https://www.dogstrust.org.uk/dog-advice/training/basics/sit-training) identifies the bottom reaching the floor as the behavior to mark. The 500ms game scoring window, virtual skill estimates and point changes are authored game rules, not measurements of a real dog's learning or neurobiology.

Stay/wait reward delivery preserves the held posture. New enrollment requires a name, breed and explicit sex; birth date is optional, neuter status is nullable, and clinical identifiers, weight, clinic, photos and care timestamps are not copied from the selected companion. Existing true/false neuter records retain their supplied values.

Verification scripts: `scripts/test-training-walkthrough.cjs` exercises all eight positive actions, an early marker that still permits food, cancellation/reload, down/fetch at four viewports, reduced motion and unknown enrollment/reload. Response randomness is controlled only around the cue, not while Three.js creates resources. `scripts/test-training-interruptions.cjs` is the independent review reproducer for Pet, hidden-page and dog-change cancellation plus the procedural Bangal fallback. Browser captures use CDP without clipping/beyond-viewport capture: the earlier capture option resized the viewport and unmounted the desktop Coach, so that invalid capture was rejected and replaced.

Primary stay reference: [Dogs Trust stay training](https://www.dogstrust.org.uk/dog-advice/training/basics/stay-training). Domain tests distinguish premature food from waiting for a non-response; the former earns no patient-waiting credit. An early marker never disables the promised reward.
